THE SIGNATURE DIFFERENTIATOR

DAEMON Proof-Loop™

Proof before production. ACE should not ask you to trust an AI-generated change blindly. It should show what it proposed, what was tested, what passed, what remains uncertain and what it expects to change.

POWERED BY PRE-FLIGHT TWIN™

Test the change in a twin. Not on your customers.

Before an authorized production change, the intended design is evaluated in an isolated test environment that approximates the relevant infrastructure state. The twin's fidelity, data freshness and test coverage are disclosed—because a simulation is useful only when its limits are understood.

01 / MIRROR —Capture approved configuration, dependencies and relevant runtime signals.
02 / CHALLENGE —Run defined load, failure, policy and security test scenarios in isolation.
03 / COMPARE —Compare baseline and proposed behavior, capacity and modeled cost.
04 / EXPLAIN —Show evidence, test coverage, assumptions and unresolved risks.
05 / APPROVE —Require an authorized human decision before production execution.
06 / VERIFY —Observe the rollout, check agreed health signals and preserve an audit trail.
THE RECEIPT OF SAFETY

Give decision-makers proof they can understand.

Replace opaque AI output with a concise change record: what ACE intends to do, the evidence behind the recommendation, the risks it found, the estimated impact and the exact approval being requested. The Receipt of Safety is designed to support internal security reviews, change-control decisions and audit preparation with traceable findings, assumptions, scope and human-review history—not to certify compliance or guarantee an audit outcome.

ENGINEERING TRANSPARENCY & SCOPE
Clear evidence. Honest boundaries. Human-controlled change.
DAEMON ACE is an actively developing engineering platform. The public demo uses illustrative scenarios and modeled metrics—not customer results, measured savings, live security findings or live infrastructure execution. The free YAML X-Ray uses lightweight text-pattern heuristics; it is not a full YAML parser, Kubernetes admission controller, vulnerability scanner, penetration test or compliance assessment. The Receipt of Safety organizes available scope, findings, assumptions and review evidence; it does not certify compliance or guarantee an audit outcome.
Product interfaces and ecosystem integrations are delivered in stages and are not represented as connected, certified or production-ready until verified. Human approval records a decision for manual review; the automated production deployment endpoint is disabled. Any implementation work is a separately scoped, human-led service subject to customer authorization, change controls, validation and rollback planning. Managed monitoring, incident response and 24/7 SLA coverage require confirmed staffing, integrations and explicit contract terms. Market estimates are third-party research, not DAEMON ACE revenue, customer traction or market share. Do not submit secrets, tokens, private keys or confidential customer data to the public X-Ray.
